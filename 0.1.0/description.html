TAS Leaderboard + Editor

Adds a Show TAS button next to "Only verified" on the leaderboard screen. While it is on, the leaderboard and the run replays come from a community TAS leaderboard (taslb.jadestone.dev) instead of the official one. Click a row and Play to watch that run in the real game.

Press E (rebindable under Controls > TAS) on a custom track to open a TAS editor; press E again to run your edited inputs in the game as a ghost. The editor is loaded from 127.0.0.1:8780 in a sandboxed frame.

What it sends, and where

  • While Show TAS is on, leaderboard and recording requests (track id, paging) go to taslb.jadestone.dev instead of Kodub's servers. Nothing else is redirected.
  • Submitting a run is off by default (Settings > TAS). When on, it asks every time and shows the server, track, frames and name. It sends your nickname, country and car style and a separate random TAS token. Your PolyTrack account token is never read or sent, and TAS runs are never sent to Kodub's servers.
  • Custom tracks only. Runs are verified by the leaderboard host.
